# Halloween media sources for Lumina Live — 2026

Researched **2026-09-10–2026-09-11 (Asia/Tokyo)**. Research-only handoff; no media saved. Parent owns acquisition, editing, browser testing and publishing.

## Result and practical shortlist

**13 selected clips, approximately 250.61 MB total for all original files**, below the 500 MB acquisition ceiling. Four are CC0; three CC BY; five CC BY-SA; one has an official USGS public-domain statement. The exact observed URLs, per-item author/rights evidence, estimates, credits and acquisition status are in `halloween-candidates.json`.

The strongest starting set is **moon-360 + vetla-fog + misty-river-47**: three CC0 sources totaling approximately **91.03 MB**. Add **full-moon-clouds** when attribution and ShareAlike for adaptations are acceptable. `misty-river` is an alternate to `misty-river-47`, not a promise of a distinct visual style; compare them before including both. The small Ljubljana moon shots offer inexpensive variation with attribution.

This is an **atmospheric source collection**, dominated by real fog and moon footage. **No polished pumpkin / haunted-house / flying-bat-swarm loop with both unambiguous bundle redistribution rights and a verified downloadable payload was established.** `pumpkin-carving` is genuine Halloween footage but only 480×272 and documentary in style. `bats-thermal` is low-resolution thermal surveillance involving a turbine. Both are reserve inserts, not hero backgrounds. Do not describe this collection as 13 purpose-made Halloween loops.

Recommendations are based on publisher descriptions, dimensions and licenses. No clip was played through, decoded, frame-reviewed, tested for seamless looping, or tested in Lumina Live.

## Clip-specific assessment

- **moon-360:** A simple rotating moon for dark backgrounds; rotation does not prove a seamless loop. Evidence: Own work; CC0 dedication in the file Licensing section.
- **vetla-fog:** Fog-covered village and woods; strong atmospheric opening. Convert OGV for browser distribution. Evidence: Own work; CC0 dedication in the file Licensing section.
- **misty-river-47:** Mist crossing a wooded riverbank; silent; suitable as an eerie background after grading. Evidence: Own work; CC0 dedication; explicitly described as silent.
- **lases-fog:** Moving lake mist; retain attribution and license for any distributed adaptation; omit audio for band use. Evidence: Own work; author applies CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **obudu-fog:** Small short fog passage; 4:3 composition; use letterboxing or a documented crop. Evidence: Own work; author applies CC BY-SA 4.0.
- **falling-fog:** Long hillside fog movement; select a short section for spooky woodland atmosphere. Evidence: Own work; multiple licenses offered; select CC BY-SA 3.0 (not GFDL or Free Art License).
- **full-moon-clouds:** Full moon through clouds; especially relevant to haunted-night backgrounds. Evidence: Own work; select the offered CC BY-SA 3.0 option.
- **moon-phases:** Brief moon-phase animation. Page also says provided by NASA; retain that provenance rather than claiming St. John originated the underlying NASA imagery. Evidence: Commons records Flickr CC BY 2.0 review on 2021-06-01; source https://www.flickr.com/photos/jsjgeology/50642191866/.
- **misty-river:** Alternate foggy-river shot by the same author on the same date; compare with misty-river-47 before using both. Evidence: Own work; CC0 dedication in the file Licensing section.
- **ljubljana-moon-june:** Very small 1080p moon shot; assess noise and framing during parent download review. Evidence: Commons records source YouTube license reviewed by MZaplotnik on 2025-08-08; CC BY 3.0.
- **ljubljana-moon-september:** Second inexpensive moon shot; alternate date and framing; assess visually before including both. Evidence: Commons records source YouTube license reviewed by MZaplotnik on 2025-11-15; CC BY 3.0.
- **pumpkin-carving:** Actual Halloween pumpkin picking/carving; documentary footage, not a polished VJ loop. Review for suitable pumpkin-only portions and remove audio before band use. Evidence: Own work; CC BY-SA 3.0 and public VRT confirmation ticket 2013120610014427.
- **bats-thermal:** Thermal surveillance of a bat around a wind turbine at night; unusual ghostly monochrome insert, not a clean silhouette swarm. Credit USGS without implying endorsement. Source PD statement is US-government provenance, not a worldwide CC0 dedication. Evidence: Sources/Usage: Public Domain.

For Commons own-work entries, the file page is the author's publication and license grant. The two Ljubljana files are reviewed imports: their Commons pages record YouTube license review by MZaplotnik. `moon-phases` is a reviewed Flickr import; its description additionally credits NASA. Keep that provenance and use the explicitly stated CC BY 2.0 option rather than silently relabeling it CC0. See [its file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:James_St._John_-_Moon_phases_%26_libration_over_1.5_months_%28video%29.webm).

## Redistribution and credits

- **CC0:** copying, adaptation, redistribution and performance are permitted, including commercially. Credits are optional but useful for provenance. [CC0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- **CC BY:** retain title, creator, source/license links and relevant notices; identify adaptations. These licenses permit sharing files, so they fit an attributed browser media bundle. [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
- **CC BY-SA:** preserve attribution and the selected license. When distributing a trimmed, recolored, composited or otherwise adapted clip, apply the applicable ShareAlike terms to that adaptation and document changes. Treat each media file's license separately from the application license; a collection does not erase its constituent licenses. [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- **USGS:** the specific source labels this footage Public Domain. Credit the agency and avoid implying endorsement; Paul Cryan is the page's contact, not a verified camera-operator credit. This is US-government public-domain provenance, not a worldwide CC0 dedication. [Specific source](https://www.usgs.gov/media/videos/bats-wind-turbines).

Do not rely on the non-profit nature of the band to justify file redistribution. Keep an accessible media-credits view or bundled notices, including the original source and license. The JSON's `suggestedCredit` values are templates: replace the changes placeholder with actual operations before release. A license permits an action; it does not establish decoded video quality.

## Sources excluded from raw bundling

- **Pixabay:** current terms prohibit standalone distribution. A free download or non-profit stage use does not establish the right to redistribute the source clip inside an app library. [Official license summary](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). Relevant examples reviewed: [Halloween / pumpkin / bat / house](https://pixabay.com/videos/halloween-pumpkin-bat-house-40031/), [purple Halloween ghosts and pumpkins](https://pixabay.com/videos/halloween-ghost-pumpkin-312689/), [AGP Studios pumpkin animation](https://pixabay.com/videos/halloween-pumpkin-autumn-harvest-307783/). The last has a CC0 phrase in descriptive text but also the Pixabay Content License; treat the conflict as unresolved.
- **Pexels:** current official guidance states standalone sale or distribution is prohibited. [Official terms explanation](https://help.pexels.com/hc/en-us/articles/900005880463-What-are-the-Terms-and-Conditions). Search results titled “public domain Halloween” are not license evidence.
- **Beachfront Moon Close Up:** Commons lists reviewed CC BY 3.0, but its description also restricts offering it as stock footage; omitted in favor of less ambiguous moon sources. [File page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Moon_Close_Up_%28Free_to_Use_Stock_Video_Footage%29.webm).
- Film/cartoon excerpts, franchise imagery, NC/ND material, paid packs, login-only sources and unreviewed YouTube reposts were not adopted.

## Parent handoff

1. Start with the three preferred CC0 IDs; validate access through legitimate source download routes. If HTTP access is refused, record it and skip rather than bypass.
2. Download only chosen files and enforce **<500,000,000 source bytes**. All 13 currently total about 251 MB, leaving roughly 249 MB headroom; do not assume held items fit.
3. Inspect framing, brightness, people/logos, audio and motion after acquisition. The pumpkin documentary may contain unsuitable portions; no footage-level clearance was claimed.
4. Prepare muted browser delivery variants. OGV/Theora originals should be transcoded; verify H.264 MP4 or supported WebM decoding in the target browsers. Container names alone do not prove the codec. Test loop seams and any crossfades.
5. Preserve source/license metadata and actual edit history. Host selected files with the app's HTTPS media path after the parent completes its own implementation and checks.
